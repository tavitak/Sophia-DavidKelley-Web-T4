Adoption, Foster, and Volunteer Services

Adoption Process

Every animal in our care receives a health check, vaccinations, and spay or neuter surgery before adoption. We place dogs, cats, and small animals such as guinea pigs and rabbits.

  1. Browse our adoptable animals and choose one you would like to meet.
  2. Submit an interest form and select adoption information.
  3. Meet the animal and talk with an adoption counselor about your home and schedule.
  4. Sign the adoption agreement and take your new companion home.

Foster Program

Foster families give animals a quiet place to recover from surgery, grow up, or learn to trust people before adoption. Most foster placements last two to eight weeks.

We provide food, supplies, and all veterinary care at no cost to foster families. Our foster coordinator checks in regularly and is available for questions.

Volunteer Roles

Animal Care Volunteer

Feed animals, clean living areas, and walk dogs at our care center. Shifts are about two hours.

Cat Socializer

Spend quiet time with shy cats so they become comfortable with people and ready for adoption.

Adoption Event Volunteer

Greet families, answer questions, and help animals stay calm at weekend adoption events.

Transport Volunteer

Drive animals to veterinary appointments and to foster homes. A valid driver's license is required.
