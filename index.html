Every Rescue Animal Deserves a Second Chance

Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue rescues homeless and surrendered animals, gives them the care they need, and places them in safe, loving homes. With help from volunteers, foster families, donors, and community partners, we work toward a Twin Cities where every animal has a place to belong.

Our Impact

  • 1,250 animals placed in adoptive homes since our founding
  • 180 active foster families caring for animals in their homes
  • 320 volunteers giving their time each year
  • 95 percent of adopted animals still in their homes after one year

How You Can Help

  • Adopt: Give a rescue animal a permanent home. Learn about our adoption process.
  • Foster: Care for an animal in your home while it waits for adoption. Read about our foster program.
  • Volunteer: Walk dogs, socialize cats, or help at adoption events. Submit a volunteer or foster interest form.
  • Donate or partner: Support food, supplies, and veterinary care. Meet our community partners.
