About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began when a small group of neighbors started fostering stray cats in their own homes. As more animals needed help, the group grew into a community nonprofit supported by volunteers, foster families, and local donors.

Today we rescue dogs, cats, and small animals from across the Twin Cities metro area and match each one with a family that fits its needs.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal and every person with kindness and respect.
  • Trust: We are honest with adopters about each animal's health and behavior.
  • Community: We rely on volunteers, fosters, donors, and partners to do this work together.
  • Action: We move quickly to help animals in need.

Our Volunteers

Volunteers walk and socialize animals, drive pets to veterinary visits, and welcome families at adoption events.
